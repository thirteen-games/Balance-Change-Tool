Balance Change Tool

Pull win/loss data from BigQuery for any date range, then sort and filter the 199-card master list by Buff/Rework rating and Overall rating to find candidates for balance changes.

1. Pull from BigQuery

Set the parameters, sign in with your Google account, and click Run query. The tool runs two queries (wins + losses) against market-party-289715.nova_island_analytics_prod.match and fills the data below automatically.
