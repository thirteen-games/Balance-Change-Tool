Balance Change Tool

Paste win and loss counts from your SQL query. The tool joins them against the master card list (199 cards), then computes a Buff/Rework rating and an Overall rating for each card.

1. Paste data

Each line is card name followed by a number, separated by tab, comma, pipe, or multiple spaces. Headers are auto-stripped. Names are matched case-insensitively against the master list.
