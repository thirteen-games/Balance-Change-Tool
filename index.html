Balance Change Tool

Paste or upload card win/loss data to identify candidates for buffs, nerfs, and reworks.

1. Input data

Expected columns (CSV or TSV, header row required): card, wins, losses. Extra columns are kept and shown as additional table columns.

2. Thresholds

Cards are categorized based on win rate and total games played. Adjust the thresholds to match your balance philosophy.
